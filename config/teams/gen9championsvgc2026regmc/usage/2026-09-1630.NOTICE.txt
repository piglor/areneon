2026-09-1630.json: attribution and basis

This file is a derived table of aggregate usage statistics published by Smogon University
(https://www.smogon.com/stats/) for the format gen9championsvgc2026regmc, month 2026-09, rating
cutoff 1630, 1,631,943 battles. The statistics are Smogon's and credit for them is theirs.
The inputs are named in the table's sources block by URL, retrieval time, size, SHA-256 and
attribution text; config/opponent-model/gen9championsvgc2026regmc/smogon-chaos.2026-09-1630.inputs.json
keeps the same facts. The raw files are not in this repository; anyone can download them and re-derive
this table byte for byte with scripts/derive_usage_weights.py.

What the table holds: per species, integer weights (parts per million of that species' own list) of
items, abilities, moves, Stat Point spreads with natures, and teammates, truncated to the stated top-K
limits, with the truncated and unmapped mass stated per list. It holds no team, replay, log or player
name, and the key evs is never used.

Rights basis: Smogon publishes no licence or terms for these files (docs/research/data-sources-rights.md,
D5). The project owner accepted the risk of using the aggregate statistics without a grant (ADR-009
erratum 1; ADR-020 section 4). This is a recorded risk, not a legal opinion, and no claim is made that
the numbers are public domain. If Smogon objects, this table is replaced by a derivation without it.
