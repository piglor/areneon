# Damage kernel (ADR-030 step 3)

A clean-room integer implementation of the Champions damage computation behind the mechanics probe's interface, the CPU reference of ADR-030 step 3. It answers `damage` mechanics queries (the `rows` and `threats` arrays of the feature builders) with the probe's exact bytes, for **training games only** and only for content admitted by byte-equality with the pinned probe through the differential harness ([differential.md](differential.md)). The probe stays the definition and the evaluation path (ADR-016 as amended by ADR-030). Ticket figures and verdicts are on Redmine #1457.

Part of the architecture documentation split out of `CLAUDE.md` (index: [README](README.md)).

**Not wired.** No training run, loader or evaluation path uses the kernel in this step: `generation.backends`, routing in the batch seam, per-entity fallback counts in run metrics and the per-generation shadow check (ADR-030 common rules 4 and 5) are the next part of #1457. A source scan (`packages/team-dataset/test/differential-kernel.test.ts`) requires that only the kernel's own modules, the bridge index, the differential composition root and its script name it.

**Modules** (`packages/showdown-bridge/src/`):

- `damage-kernel.ts`: `prepareQuery` decodes one `damage` query (the probe envelope's validated sets) into the numbers `getDamage` reads, or a finished outcome (`unsupported` `query_kind` for a status, multi-hit, fixed-damage or OHKO move, or a move the record marks as failing; all-zero vectors for an immunity), or throws `KernelAbstain` naming the entity it does not implement (`<kind>:<id>`, a fixed `why`). `computeBatch` runs the roll loop of a batch over struct-of-arrays `Int32Array` columns (base damage, final-modifier chain, flags, weather, same-type and type modifiers): 16 no-crit and 16 crit rolls per query. Integers only: every division is exact integer division, every modifier the engine's 4096-based integer (`modify`, `chain` = `chainModify`). `KERNEL_FORMAT` (2) is bumped when it computes anything differently or covers more.
- `damage-kernel-data.ts`: the pinned engine's DATA for the format's mod, read once per engine and format (`kernelData`): species base stats and types, natures, the type chart, moves' damage fields, Mega Stones, hook ordering siblings, `breakable`. **Inertness by reflection:** an ability, item or move is inert when none of its data keys is a hook the probe's damage path can reach; a hook is any key starting `on` or ending `Callback` (constants such as Battle Armor's `onCriticalHit: false` included; ordering siblings such as `onModifyAtkPriority` excluded), also inside `condition`, `secondary`, `secondaries` and `self`. `INERT_HOOKS` is the reviewed list of hook names that cannot change a `damage` answer in the probe's flow (after-hit, residual, switching, speed, accuracy, PP, healing, item-taking, trapping, boost-application and volatile-adding hooks, a switch-in's `onStart`, whose stages, item and field the declared state overwrites); `STATUS_HOOKS` are inert only without a declared status on the holder. `simLiterals` scans the pinned simulator sources for ids the code tests by name (`hasItem('ironball')`, `hasAbility('levitate')`, `move.id === 'facade'`, volatiles by name, `.includes` lists): such an id is non-inert whatever its hooks.
- `damage-kernel-effects.ts` and `config/backends/<format>.damage-kernel.effects.json`: the reviewed effects record. The kernel holds effect KINDS (how each step of `getDamage` applies one: `pinch`, `base_power_flag`, `stab`, `no_crit`, `guts`, `attack_multiplier`, `source_type_attack`, `technician`, `super_effective_taken`, `full_hp_taken`, `crit_damage`, `defense_multiplier`, `ground_immunity`, `suppress_weather`, `mold_breaker`, `absorb`, `flag_immunity`, `infiltrator`, `priority_block`, `ate`, `sheer_force`, `unnerve`, `type_boost`, `resist_berry`, `final_modifier`, `super_effective_dealt`, `category_power`, `crit_ratio`, `status_power`, `constant_power`, `boost_power`, `needs_sleep`, `fails`, `no_damage_effect`); which ability, item or move has which kind, and its type, flag or 4096-based modifier, is data, never code. The record is bound to the pin (`showdown_commit`); another pin's record makes the kernel abstain on every query (`state:effects_record`).
- `damage-kernel-backend.ts`: `kernelBackend(engine, {config, snapshot?, backend_commit})` is the kernel behind the probe interface, a level-1 `Backend` of the harness: a batch of parsed queries in, outcomes in order. The result envelope (identity, set check, scope check, scenario check) is the probe's own (`probeEnvelope` in `mechanics-probe.ts`, factored out unchanged), so a result differs only in what the kernel computed. A query of another kind and every abstention is a `KernelAbstain` outcome; `counts` holds answers and abstentions by entity (the per-entity fallbacks run metrics will report).

**What KERNEL_FORMAT 2 covers:** the set's species forme (a Mega or battle-only forme as a set's own species abstains) or the Mega forme its stone makes; Champions Stat Point stats; absolute stages; declared HP and status; Physical and Special moves with a fixed base power and single or spread targets, with `willCrit`, `critRatio`, the stat overrides, `ignoreDefensive` and `ignoreImmunity` from data; type, absorbing and flag immunities and Psychic Terrain's priority block (all-zero vectors); rain, sun, sand and snow, the four terrains, Reflect, Light Screen and Aurora Veil, Trick Room and Tailwind, burn; the effects record's entries; a declared history with no volatile and no pending slot condition whose facts the engine would accept (used moves of the set, engine moves and items, no attacker list or stall counter: the probe refuses or checks those against its own state). **Event order:** the handlers an event finds are ordered as the engine orders them (order, priority, speed, sub-order, from the data's ordering siblings); two Pokemon's handlers tying on order and priority (their speeds decide, or the engine draws) or two conditions tying on equal sub-orders, the same side's included (the engine draws their order: two screens on one side), make the kernel abstain (`state:handler_tie`).

**Admission** (`packages/team-dataset/src/differential-kernel.ts`, the composition root; `scripts/differential-kernel-admission.ts`). An abstained query routes to the engine and is no evidence either way, so only COVERED queries are compared and tallied (the harness would read an abstention as a difference); coverage is counted beside the tally. Queries are the ones the feature builders issue (`collectQueries`, `probe-damage/2` and `probe-outcome/1`). Regression corpora: the committed random, search-fidelity and unavailable corpora (debugging evidence, never admission). The fresh corpus: seeded random-legal battles on the generator pool (the `fixtures-v1` dev split, Illusion excluded as the run configs do), battle `i` drawn from `fresh-<freshSeed(kernel)>` (`freshBattle`), so it cannot exist before the kernel's commit is frozen; the first 200,000 distinct covered damage queries in battle order. `differential-kernel-admission.ts fresh --commit SHA --shard K/S` plays shards in parallel (it refuses unless the kernel files equal the frozen commit's), `ledger` merges them in battle order (only battles every shard finished), compares the kernel with the recorded probe answers, adds the committed corpora as regression corpora and writes the `BackendAdmission` ledger (`config/backends/<format>.damage-kernel.admission.json`) and a counts report; `regression` prints coverage, differences and abstentions per committed corpus; `bench` times the kernel and the probe on the same queries.

**Tests:** `packages/showdown-bridge/test/damage-kernel.test.ts` (ADR-016's hand-worked damage fixtures covered and equal; targeted queries for every entry of the effects record, as attacker and defender, across six field states, statuses, HP and stages; data rules; history; batch order; every abstention rule; the stat formula, stages, the record's reader, reflection and the simulator scan); `packages/team-dataset/test/differential-kernel.test.ts` (zero differences on the first battles of each committed corpus with a coverage floor; negative controls: a perturbed roll is caught at `$.answer.no_crit[3]` naming the move, and a kernel that answered in place of an abstention is caught; a small fresh corpus's ledger is read by the reader and admits nothing below 200,000 items; the committed ledger is this pin's; the isolation scan). Both were run first against a stub kernel (every roll 0) and failed (commit `238b0c04`), then against the kernel and passed.

**Admission results at pin `7244fe8e`** (Redmine #1457 has the logs). Format 1 (frozen at `f1237433`) had zero differences on the committed corpora but its fresh corpus found 20 in two classes: Magician's `onAfterMoveSecondarySelf` draws with the engine's random source (the probe answers `random_effect`), and a declared status refused by a terrain a switch-in's Surge started (the probe throws). Format 2 abstains on both (the after-move hook is no longer inert; a status beside an ability that starts a condition with a status hook, from the committed mechanics inventory's `starts`, abstains); format 1's fresh corpus is regression evidence for it, never admission. Format 2 (frozen at `213c6b01`; the only change since is the effects record's location, moved byte for byte out of `config/mechanics/` so the mechanics config checker does not read it, and the one path constant in `damage-kernel-effects.ts`): fresh corpus of 200,000 distinct covered damage queries from 446 seeded battles, **zero differences**, nothing revoked; regression corpora random 22,359 / 37,808 covered, search-fidelity 85,051 / 164,362, unavailable 769 / 2,204, all equal; **791 entities admitted** of 1,143 listed (species 294, move 195, ability 148, item 134, status 6, weather 4, terrain 4, side 4, field 1, kind 1), **47,649 of 60,202 listed pairs admitted**. The ledger is `config/backends/gen9championsvgc2026regmc.damage-kernel.admission.json` (JCS bytes, `admission_hash` `78004682...`). Coverage: the kernel covers about 53% of the builders' damage queries by count and about 52% of the probe's damage time.

**Speed** (`differential-kernel-admission.ts bench`, 10,000 damage queries of the committed random corpus, one thread, nice 19, a shared machine at load 7): kernel per covered query median 0.123 ms, p99 0.525 ms; the probe on the same queries median 2.41 ms, p99 11.75 ms (21x on the mean). Projection (ADR-030's shares: rows and threats 53% of the builder, the builder 45 to 65% of play, play 75% of a generation): builder time falls to about 0.74 (1.36x), a generation to about 0.87 to 0.91 of its time. **ADR-030's speed gate (builder 1.5x, `T'/T` at most 0.85) is not met at this coverage**; it needs about 75% of the probe's damage time covered. The kernel's per-query time includes the probe's envelope (query hash, validated-set memo), so most of its 0.12 ms is not the roll loop.
