# Battle-state tensors

The battle-state tensor schema: the reviewed, versioned list of fields a batched battle-state tensor holds for one format, with each field's source in the pinned engine's state, the per-seat bookkeeping of what each seat was shown, the visibility classes and projection rules, and the map from today's reducer and encoder to the fields.

Part of the architecture documentation split out of `CLAUDE.md` (index: [README](README.md)). Ticket references such as `#1463` point to Redmine (`redmine.piglor.com/projects/areneon`).

**What it is, and what it is not** (ADR-030 step 5 groundwork, ADR-028 section 5, #1463). ADR-028's direct tensor path needs "a projection of the surrogate's full state ... a reviewed, versioned list of fields", and ADR-030 step 5 needs that projection to give exactly `Encoder.encode`'s tensors with privacy by construction. This is the list, written from what exists (the engine's `Battle.toJSON()`, the world facts, the shared reader, the reducer and the encoder), as data: `config/engine-state/<format>/battle-state-spec.v1.json`, the `BattleStateSpec` contract (`packages/schema/contracts/v1/battle-state-spec.schema.json`, generated TS and pydantic types, samples in `fixtures/contracts/v1/battle-state-spec/`). **No engine is built and no tensor is produced here** (that is G4, #1464). The spec is an input to step 5's build ADR, not a commitment to the direct tensor path: ADR-028 sections 4 and 5 still require channels or a forced-draw trace before any surrogate game is training data. On a pin bump (Regulation M-D) the vocabulary sizes and any new state keys change; the drift test below shows exactly which.

**Readers.** TS `packages/showdown-bridge/src/battle-state-spec.ts` (`readBattleStateSpec(value, binding?)`, `loadBattleStateSpec(engine, formatId)`, `bindingFor`, `battleStateSpecHash`, `seatView`, `pathKeys`, `coveredKeys`, `resolvePath`, `vocabularyIds`) and Python `areneon_contracts.battle_state_spec` (`read_battle_state_spec(value, engine_data?)`, `battle_state_spec_hash`, `seat_view`, `path_keys`, `vocabulary_ids`, `state_item_ids`). Both refuse the same reader-invalid samples with the same words. `spec_hash` is SHA-256 of the record's JCS bytes (pinned in `battle-state-spec.test.ts` and `tests/contracts/test_battle_state_spec.py`); a change of any value is a new record, never an edit. The record names the engine-data version 2 table by `engine_data_hash` and the team vocabulary by `vocabulary_hash`, so the hash binds both.

## Groups and shapes

| Group | Shape | Holds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `battle` | () | turn, started, ended, the last move, the PRNG state (opaque, hidden), the Quick Claw roll (hidden) |
| `side` | (SIDE) | side conditions (present, layers, duration), faint counters, Pokemon left, team size |
| `roster` | (SIDE, ROSTER) | species and forme, Mega state, level, gender, HP and max HP, the five stored stats, status and its counters, item and item-lost flags, ability, four move slots with PP and flags, stages, types, per-turn flags |
| `active` | (SIDE, ACTIVE) | the roster index in each slot, volatiles (present, layers, duration, counter, HP), `activeTurns`, `activeMoveActions`, last moves, the request flags, slot conditions |
| `field` | () | weather, terrain and field conditions, with their durations |
| `seat_field` | (SEAT) | the seat's own request kind; elapsed turns of the weather, terrain and field conditions as the seat counts them |
| `seat_side` | (SEAT, SIDE) | elapsed turns of each side condition |
| `seat_roster` | (SEAT, SIDE, ROSTER) | the roster gate `seen`, the preview species, level and gender, the species mask, the last shown HP, status, item, ability, Mega and stone, the revealed moves in reveal order, brought, typechange types, the reducer's recent history and history facts |
| `seat_active` | (SEAT, SIDE, ACTIVE) | the roster entry shown in each slot, which volatiles the seat was shown, their derived suffix and start turn, the history facts (stall counter, move actions, hits, attackers, failures, stat changes), pending slot effects |

Dimensions are data, never constants of the reader: counts come from `bindingFor` (the engine's number of sides, the rule table's `maxMoveCount`, the team vocabulary's team sizes, the engine's `activePerHalf` and PRNG width, the engine's stat and boost ids) and the reader checks each against the record; a vocabulary dimension (`VOLATILE`, `SIDE_CONDITION`, `PSEUDO_WEATHER`, `SLOT_CONDITION`, `TYPE`) names engine-data v2 lists and its size is the union of their ids, read from the table. A vocabulary value is an index: 0 none, 1 unknown, then 2 + the id's position in the sorted union; the reader refuses a dtype too narrow for the table's size.

## Sources

Every field names its source. **Engine** fields name a path into `Battle.toJSON()` (roots `battle`, `side`, `pokemon`, `active` (a side's Pokemon in play: the engine serialises `side.active` as references), `field`; `[]` walks an array, `{}` an object's values) and a `read` rule (`value`, `bool`, `present`, `id`, `length`, `pokemon_ref`, `species_mega`, `details_level`, `details_shiny`, `multi_hot`, `opaque`). **Derived** fields name the module that fills them (`world_facts`, `shared_reader`, `reducer`, `reducer_history`, `engine_request`, `own_team`) and the value. A seat field is always derived: the engine's state holds nothing a seat was shown.

Engine keys at the six classified levels (battle, side, pokemon, field, move slot, effect state) that no field reads are in `omitted` with a reason (`cosmetic`, `derived`, `not_in_format`, `internal`, `hidden_input`, `reference`): for example `pokemon.set` and `ppUps` are team-building inputs held only through their effects (stats, max PP), the Terastallization, Dynamax and Z keys are not in the format, and `battle.queue` and the event machinery are the engine's transient state.

**Mega Evolution and Champions specifics.** The Mega forme is `roster.species` (the current forme) and `roster.mega` (the forme's `isMega`); the stone is the held item (`roster.item`), `roster.can_mega_evo` is the own request's flag, the pre-Evolution ability is `roster.base_ability` and the forme's is `roster.ability`. Level 50 and Stat Points enter only through `roster.level`, `roster.max_hp` and `roster.stats` (the engine's stored values); the set's Stat Points, nature and IVs are `hidden_input`. The engine's status marker `fnt` is `roster.fainted`, and the typeless marker `???` sets no type flag.

## Visibility classes and projection rules

| Class | The owner | Another seat |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `public` | exact | exact (for a gated group, only at an entry it has seen) |
| `own-exact` | exact | nothing (`projection` is `none`) |
| `revealed-only` | where its own mask is set | where that seat's mask is set; else the unknown marker |
| `hidden` | never | never |
| `seat` | exact at its own seat index | never (seat 1 never reads seat 0's bookkeeping) |

A projection reads a field only through its class (`seatView(field, {owner, seat})`, Python `seat_view(field, owner, seat)`; both take the seat explicitly): nothing can project a `hidden` field, an `own-exact` field with projection `none` never reaches another seat, and a `seat` field is read only at the reader's own seat index. A `revealed-only` field names its mask (`reveal`), a 0/1 seat field of the same entity and extra dimensions whose `masks` names exactly that field (the readers refuse a mask shared with another value). Every field of the opponent half that a mapping reads is either `public` and checked against the seat's own reader (below), or a `seat` field: the seat's own derivation.

**What another seat may read, and why the rest is `own-exact` with projection `none`.** The independent oracle (below) showed that projecting the engine's current value is not what a seat saw for: exact HP (a benched Pokemon's HP can change with no display the seat reads), status (another seat reads `seat_roster.status_shown`), the bad-poison stage (it rises silently when Magic Guard prevents the damage), the held item (an item can move with no line that names it on the holder's side), the current and the set's ability (a Mega Evolution or a swap changes it with no line naming the new one), the Mega flag (a Pokemon transformed into a Mega forme reads Mega), the engine's base species (a permanent forme change moves it; another seat reads the preview species), the move slots (another seat reads the revealed moves in reveal order: the slot order is not shown), `activeMoveActions` (the world facts' count differs on some turns), the active slot's roster index (Illusion), and the engine's per-turn flags, move results, last-move targets and history counters (no derivation of another seat is checked against them; it reads its own history facts). Each of these has a `seat` field that is the seat's own view. Public engine fields: the turn, `ended`, weather, terrain, field conditions, side conditions and their layers, total faints, and per roster entry the species forme (revealed-only), level, gender, shiny, boosts, fainted, in play and transformed, and per active slot the volatiles (revealed-only) and `activeTurns`.

**The roster gate (unseen and non-brought entries pack alike).** After Team Preview the engine holds only the brought four. The `roster` group's `gate` is `seat_roster.seen` (the seat has seen the entry in play): for another seat, an entry it has not seen packs as the unknown entry, so an unseen brought Pokemon and a non-brought one are indistinguishable. What every seat was shown at Team Preview (species, level, gender) is seat bookkeeping (`seat_roster.preview_*`).

**The Illusion rule.** While a Pokemon with Illusion is in play disguised, another seat's view packs its battle state (HP shown, boosts, volatiles, fainted, in play) at the entry it is shown as, with that entry's identity (species, level, gender, shiny), and its own entry is the seat's last view of it, which the engine's current state does not hold: nothing of the engine is packed there until the `|replace|`. This is what the reducer and the shared reader do (they pin a switch-in to the shown entry until the replace). G4's packer takes `packOpponent` in `packages/showdown-bridge/test/battle-state-spec.test.ts` as the reference implementation of this rule and of the roster gate: it is the code the oracle holds equal to the reader on every committed request, the Illusion battle included. `active.roster_index` is `own-exact` with projection `none`; another seat reads `seat_active.shown_roster_index`.

No field is projected through a display rule: what another seat was shown of hit points is the seat field `seat_roster.hp_shown` (the Champions display, `floor(100 * hp / max)` with a Pokemon that has hit points left never at 0, is the shared reader's to apply), since a benched Pokemon's hit points can change with no display that seat reads.

**Engine facts checked for the review (#1463, PR #411).** At this pin the Champions mod overrides Natural Cure (`onCheckShow` removed, a `-curestatus ... [silent]` line on every switch-out cure) and Regenerator (a `-heal ... [silent]` line), so neither hides its effect from the protocol here; status and HP are still `own-exact` with projection `none`, since the oracle found benched HP changes no seat reads and the mod overrides may not hold on another pin. The toxic stage does rise under Magic Guard with no line (`data/conditions.ts` `tox.onResidual` raises the stage before the damage Magic Guard cancels).

## Encoder and reducer mapping

`encoder` maps every state column of the encoder's `Layout` (`mon_cat`, `mon_num`, `field_cat`, `field_num`, `cand_cat`, `cand_tok`; `#` stands for a number, `*` for an id) to the spec fields it is a function of, per half: `own` (an own token, or the own side's columns of the field token), `opponent`, `shared` (fields without a side) and `query` (the column also reads the candidate or the seat's own request). The covered layouts are the base layout, the lineage run's options (`candidate_matchup`, `recent_history`, `history_facts`) and `exact_volatiles`. `reducer` maps every `BattleState.to_dict` path (sides as `SIDE`, list entries as `[]`, id-keyed maps as `*`). Candidate features, the opponent sheet and the candidate target are `out_of_scope`: they are not battle state. The reader refuses any mapping of a hidden field and any opponent or shared mapping of a field another seat cannot see.

## World-writer state items

Every state item of `config/search/world-writers.v1.json` (`STATE_ITEM_IDS`) is `held` (the fields that hold it: the turn, faint counters, the item and ability changes, last moves, the per-turn flags, the volatiles with their durations and counters, the sleep and toxic counters, slot conditions, opponent PP, the bench order) or `not_held` with a reason: `unavailable_mechanics` (Terastallization, Z-Moves, Dynamax and Ultra Burst are not mechanics of the format).

## Drift and completeness gate

- `packages/showdown-bridge/test/battle-state-spec.test.ts` replays every committed corpus (the random-play corpus, `unavailable/`, the Illusion, Baton Pass, Fling, side-guard and revival battles and the targeted fidelity corpus; about 250 battles, 8,500 requests, both seats) with a spy on the engine's `BattleStream` and reads `toJSON()` at each request: every key at a classified level is a field's or omitted (an unknown key fails, and an omitted key no state holds fails as stale); every engine source resolves on real states; every vocabulary value is an id of its lists; the stat and boost ids, the move, active and side counts and the PRNG width are the binding's. **The independent oracle:** at every request of both seats, every field classed `public` or `revealed-only`, projected for the other seat with the roster gate and the Illusion rule, must equal what that seat's own shared reader or world facts show (a field with no comparison fails); a record with a field deliberately reclassified as public (`roster.hp`, `roster.status`, `roster.move_pp`, `roster.item`) fails it (negative control); every shared-reader name a seat field derives from exists in the reader's state; the roster gate is the oracle's own packing (`packOpponent` packs nothing at an entry the seat has not seen), and the corpus holds over a thousand such entries. **The canary** plants markers in every value another seat may not see (stats, max and current HP, PP, move ids and the disabled and used flags, volatile durations, counters, HP and layers, status and its counters, the item, ability, set ability, last item, Illusion target, `canMegaEvo`, bench order, times attacked, move actions, the PRNG and Quick Claw roll, and the durations of weather, terrain, field, side and slot conditions) and projects the state by the rules: no marker survives, while a projection that ignores the classes leaks (negative control). The reader's refusals and the binding are tested there too.
- `tests/ml/model/test_battle_state_spec.py`: every state column of the three layouts matches exactly one entry of an option the layout has (a dropped entry or a wrong option fails), no entry is stale, every reducer key at every request of the committed channels (both seats; the targeted fidelity corpus included) maps, and the reducer's own opponent-secret rule agrees with the spec: where the spec maps the opponent half to nothing over fields another seat does not see exactly (stats, `moves_own`, `pp`, `can_mega_evo`, `moves_used`), the reducer's opponent half holds nothing; doctored mappings are refused by the reader.
- `tests/contracts/test_battle_state_spec.py`: the Python reader on the samples and the committed record.

**How equality with today's encoder is checked (G4, below).** For every request of both seats of every committed corpus: pack `toJSON()` plus the seat bookkeeping into spec tensors with a CPU reference packer, project for the seat, rebuild the reducer-state view the encoder reads, and require the state part of `Encoder.encode` bit-equal to encoding the real `PolicyQuery` built from the Showdown channel.

## Record v2 (#1464)

`config/engine-state/<format>/battle-state-spec.v2.json` sits beside v1, which is unchanged (its hash stays pinned); v2 is the projection's record (spec hash `210ee696...`, pinned in `battle-state-spec.test.ts`). It keeps every v1 field as it was (only the descriptions of `seat_active.volatile_shown` and `seat_roster.preview_species` say more (the former's semantics changed with them: it now holds the bare id of a volatile the seat saw, the numbered and stat forms having their own fields), and the roster values held at the real index (`roster.boosts`, `roster.fainted`, `roster.is_active`, `roster.transformed`) are `own-exact` with projection `none`, because an Illusion user would show through the entry it is shown as: another seat reads `active.boosts` (the slot's occupant), `seat_active.shown_roster_index` and the new `seat_roster.fainted_shown`; and `active.volatile_counter` is int16 with range 0..1023: the engine stall counter reaches its `counterMax` of 729, which v1's 0..127 cannot hold; the field is hidden and never projected) and every mapping ref (but the other side's reads of those four roster fields), and adds what G4 found v1 could not rebuild the encoder's input from:

1. **The own Pokemon that were not brought.** After Team Preview the engine holds the brought four, but the reducer keeps the other two as the preview request showed them and the encoder tokens all six. New seat fields from the seat's own requests: `seat_roster.own_stats`, `own_moves`, `own_pp` (-1 never shown), `own_can_mega_evo` and `hp_den_shown` (the denominator of `hp_shown`).
2. **The own base species over a Mega Evolution.** The engine's `baseSpecies` moves with a Mega Evolution; the reducer keeps the set's base species. The projection reads `seat_roster.preview_species` for the own side too (the mappings say so).
3. **Raw volatile ids with their numbered derivatives.** The encoder's bucketed volatile columns hash the reducer's raw ids, which accumulate and go stale (`stockpile1`..`3`, `perish3`..`0`); `volatile_shown` now marks the bare id held, and `seat_active.volatile_numbered` (bit n: `<id>n`), `volatile_stat_raw` (bit i: `<id><stat i>`), `protocol_volatiles` and `protocol_derived` (the reducer's protocol words, which no engine-data list holds, bit layout in the descriptions) keep the raw set exactly; `volatile_started_count` and `volatile_oldest_started` give the history facts' count and oldest start, whatever their keys.
4. **A fifth revealed move.** A reveal list is at most four slots plus Struggle: `seat_roster.revealed_extra` and `revealed_extra_at` hold the fifth entry and its place.

Besides: `active.boosts` (public, the stages of the Pokemon in each slot: another seat reads them at the entry it was shown in the slot, so a disguised Pokemon's stages sit at its disguise's entry without reading the slot's real roster index), `seat_roster.ability_suppressed` and `seat_active.own_moves_used`. The oracle of the spec test runs on v2 too (`active.boosts` against the reader's stages at the shown entry).

## The reference packer and the projection (G4, #1464)

`ml/areneon_ml/projection/` (Python: the seat bookkeeping is the reducer's and its history facts', which only Python has). **Packer** (`packer.py`, `pack`, `PackSession`): a point is the engine's `toJSON()` at a request (or trimmed to the keys the record reads, `trimState` in `packages/showdown-bridge/src/projection-points.ts`) plus both seats' channels up to it and both teams as brought. The engine groups are read by each field's path and read rule (a roster entry is the side's team as brought, joined by name; an active slot by position); the seat groups from each seat's own reducer state. Struct of arrays, integers, one battle (`{"spec_hash", "fields"}`, nested lists in each field's full shape; JSON, no pickle). Refusals are `PackError` codes, never clamped: `phantom` (an Illusion twin's extra roster entry, which the ROSTER dimension has no row for: counted, not compared), `reducer_refused`, `out_of_range`, `preview_order`, `unmatched_pokemon`, `format_mismatch`, `reveal_overflow`, `unknown_volatile`. **Projection** (`project.py`, `project(index, packed, seat)`): reads a field only through its class (`seat_view` of the contracts reader): hidden has no accessor; own-exact with projection `none` reaches the owning side only; revealed-only is masked by the seat's own reveal field (null elsewhere); seat fields are the seat's own row; the roster gate makes an unseen entry of the other side the unknown entry (null). Keys are `<field>` or `<field>@own|@opponent`, so a view names no player. **Rebuild** (`view.py`, `reduced_view`): the `BattleState` the encoder reads, from the view and the seat's own team (its names) alone; `Encoder.encode_reduced(query, state)` encodes the query against it (`check.py`, `compare_encodings`: every tensor bit-equal, floats by their 32 bits; the first difference by tensor, row and column). `python -m areneon_ml.projection serve` answers the TS side's frames.

**Gate** (`packages/showdown-bridge/test/battle-state-projection-corpus-{0..3}.python.test.ts`, four shards of `test/support/projection-corpus.ts` run one at a time in their own vitest project, each driving the Python child; the invariance test and the type rule are `battle-state-projection.python.test.ts`; every committed battle the G2 recorder can replay: golden, random, unavailable, the singles (baton-pass, baton-pass-volatiles, fling, illusion, revival, side-guard) and search-fidelity; `transform/` holds no choices and is not replayable, as in G2): at every request of both seats that asks for a decision, the encoding of the projection is bit-equal to `Encoder.encode` of the seat's real query (`observe` on the Showdown channel, the trace mask's candidates) under the default options, the lineage options and `exact_volatiles`; only `phantom` (an Illusion twin) and `reveal_overflow` (an Illusion disguise that never breaks: the reducer attributes the user's moves to the Pokemon it is shown as, giving six revealed moves where the record holds four slots and one extra; 17 requests of search-fidelity b63 and b86 at this pin) may be refused, and each is counted (8,532 of 8,573 decisions equal, 24 phantom, 17 reveal_overflow). The canary at every decision: values planted in what the seat may not read (in the engine state: the other side's hidden and own-exact values, the PRNG, every duration; in the tensors: every value the classes do not let the seat read, computed from the record directly) leave its view unchanged. Invariance: battles whose opponents differ only in natures, Stat Points and held items give byte-identical views at every request with an equal player channel; a projection that adds the opponent's raw item (outside the accessor) differs (negative control). `tests/ml/projection/test_projection.py` runs the same on the committed points `fixtures/showdown/<format>/projection/points.json` (`scripts/capture-projection-points.ts`), with the negative controls (a raw-path read, a record doctored to class `roster.move_pp` and `battle.prng` public) and the codecs. Record v1 is refused by the projection, naming the fields it lacks. The recorder's `onStep` hook hands over `toJSON()` at each step end; `projection-points.ts` is the one module of the projection path that touches the recorder and the runner (a point holds no omniscient log, record or verdicts: a type test).
