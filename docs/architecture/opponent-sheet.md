# Opponent sheet block and sheet regimes

The opponent sheet input (closed and open regimes), its builders, encoder use, sidecar, the team sheet reader and open-sheet evaluation.

Part of the architecture documentation split out of `CLAUDE.md` (index: [README](README.md)). Ticket references such as `#1179` point to Redmine (`redmine.piglor.com/projects/areneon`), where measured figures and verdicts are recorded.

**Open-sheet format and the sheet-variant check** (ADR-027 section 4, section 10 slice 1, ADR-003 erratum E-3.1, #1149; `rules/formats/gen9championsvgc2026regmcbo3.json`, `packages/showdown-bridge/src/sheet-variants.ts`, `open-sheet-corpus.ts`). A second format record, the engine's Force Open Team Sheets + `Best of = 3` variant of the target, regulation M-C, `forbidden_rules: []`, `team_sheets: "open"`. The rules schema (rules v1, a minor change) gains the optional `team_sheets` (`closed` | `open`; absent means `closed`). `formatRulesRecord` puts `team_sheets: "open"` in the replay-identity record ONLY for an open format, so the CTS record keeps its bytes and `format_rules_hash` (`549cd2aa...`, pinned in `formats.test.ts` and held by committed Episodes) and an explicit `closed` is the same identity as absent. `resolveFormat` fails closed: `open` needs the engine's `forceopenteamsheets`, `closed` needs its absence. `checkSheetVariants(engine, closedId, openId)` (the `checkScenario` pattern; shares `SHARED_FIELDS` and `battleOptions` with `mechanics-scenario.ts`) requires the two rule tables to differ in exactly `SHEET_VARIANT_KEYS` = `{bestof, forceopenteamsheets, openteamsheets}` (the closed target opts into `openteamsheets` and declines it; the open one forces the sheets and carries `bestof = 3`), with the same mod, game type, battle options, rule sources, value rules (bestof aside), level and team-size fields; it throws a `SheetVariantError` naming every difference. Measured at the pin: the engine's difference is exactly that set (`bestOfDefault` is a format flag, not a rule, true on the target and false on the Bo3 format, and is not compared). `sheetVariantPairs(snapshot)` pairs each open format with the closed formats of its regulation (an open format with none is refused) and `check-rules.ts` runs the check on each pair. Gap rows: `open-team-sheets` rewritten, `open-team-sheet-contents` (the engine's `showOpenTeamSheets` writes species, item, ability, moves, gender, level and, in the champions mod, the nature; no Stat Points, IVs or nickname) and `best-of-series` (unsupported: one game of the format is played, series state is not). The `stable` channel moved to `seq` 6. **A2 refuses the format:** `decideChallenge` rejects a snapshot format with `team_sheets: open` (reason `format`; no `confirmready` path, no series) and the CLI refuses `--format` of one; the A2 and inventory defaults ("the only format") now mean the only CLOSED-sheet format. **Open-regime corpus:** `playRandomBattle(..., formatId)` and `randomBattleFile(..., provenance)` take the format; `fixtures/showdown/gen9championsvgc2026regmcbo3/open-regime/o00..o07.json` plus `manifest.json` are eight seeded battles (seeds from `0x0e000`, apart from the random corpus's) on the random corpus's first eight battles' teams; the channels hold the engine's two `|showteam|` lines before the first request. Reading `|showteam|` (the reducer raises on it today) and any sheet input to the policy are slice 2. Tests: `sheet-variants.test.ts` (the real pair, the engine's difference recomputed, doctored tables), `formats.test.ts`, `open-sheet-corpus.test.ts` (replay equality, sheets before the lead choice, sheet contents, the format's own manifest), `bot-client` `challenge.test.ts` and `session-cli.test.ts`.

**Team sheet reader and format binding** (ADR-027 section 5, slice 2, #1150; `ml/areneon_ml/reducer/sheet.py`, `packages/public-state/src/{sheet,sheet-layer}.ts`, `packages/showdown-bridge/src/team-sheet-binding.ts`). An open-sheet format makes the engine print one `|showteam|<p1|p2>|<packed>` line per side between the preview lines and the Team Preview `|request|`. **`interpreter.cjs` is byte-identical** (its SHA-256 `f6687af1...` is pinned in `packages/public-state/test/sheet.test.ts`): the replay pages inline it and `readerSha256` is part of ADR-018's baseline identity and every feature-set hash, so changing it would invalidate the run configs that name those. The TS side reads the sheet beside it: the `index.ts` wrappers (`interpretChannel`, `createInterpreter`) take every `|showteam|` line out of the stream (an inert `|` line takes its place, so frame indices, flags and request counts stay aligned; the frame of the sheet line has `kind: "showteam"`), check it in `SheetTracker` (`sheet-layer.ts`) and attach the sheets to the returned states; a channel with no sheet line goes to the interpreter untouched. The replay page does not show sheets. **Both readers** (the Python reducer and the TS wrappers, held equal by `packages/public-state/test/sheet.test.ts` over the 16 open-regime channels, both seats) give each side a `sheet` list (`SideState.sheet`; `PublicSide.sheet`, `PublicSheetMon`) of `{species, item|null, ability, moves[], nature|null, gender|null, level}`, kept apart from the roster's revealed fields (a sheet changes no item, ability or move of a roster entry; a consumed berry or Knock Off is battle state, not the sheet). The key exists only while a sheet was read, so no committed reduced snapshot, golden state or closed-game byte changed (no reduced golden was rewritten, none is added for the open channels). The packed team is parsed with a clean-room parser (Python `sheet.py`, TS `sheet.ts`) from `sim/TEAMS.md` and the committed open channels: twelve `|` fields per Pokemon (`NAME|SPECIES|ITEM|ABILITY|MOVES|NATURE|EVS|GENDER|IVS|SHINY|LEVEL|EXTRAS`), `]` between Pokemon, the line split at its second `|` only. The engine prints an unnicknamed Pokemon's species name as NAME and leaves SPECIES blank, so a non-blank SPECIES is a nickname and refused; Stat Points, IVs, shiny and extras present are refused, as are a gender other than M, F or blank, a level outside 1..100 (blank is 100), an empty move, and more than 24 Pokemon (a sanity bound, not a team-size rule). **Fail closed** (reducer: typed `ReducerError`s; TS: the line is kept and flagged): a second sheet for a side, a sheet after `|start`, an unknown side or field, and a sheet that is not the preview's (count and base species in order, opponent, once `|poke|` lines were seen); the reducer also refuses a species, item, ability or move outside the engine-data vocabulary (the TS layer holds none, so its ids come from the names; the nature has no vocabulary in the engine-data table in either reader and is checked for shape only). **Own-sheet check (reducer only, it holds the team):** the player's own sheet must equal the own team without Stat Points, IVs and nickname, in team order (species, item, ability, moves in order, level, nature when the team states one; a team gender of M or F must be shown, a blank team gender is the engine's draw and open, `N` shows no gender): a consistency check, never an input. **The Python reducer accepts a sheet in any channel** (it does not know the format's regime): safety rests on the TS gate, and the sheet is never encoded. **Format binding** (`team-sheet-binding.ts`): the regime of a format is the rules snapshot's `team_sheets` (`sheetRegimeIn`, `sheetRegimeOf(commit, format_id)`; an unknown format reads as `closed`). `playerChannel(log, team_sheets = "closed")` calls `assertSheetBinding`: a closed channel with any `|showteam|` line is refused (a spliced or mislabelled channel); an open channel needs at most one per side, before `|start` and before the first request, and exactly one for each of p1 and p2 when its first request is Team Preview (never read as a closed game). The default is `closed`, the strict reading. The regime is taken from the manifest's format by `recordedChannel`, `readEpisode`, `decisionObservations` (hence the training loader and its feature rows), `policyActor`, `searchActor` and the feature service (`sheetRegimeOf(manifest.showdown_commit, manifest.format_id)`); the hypothetical Team Preview battle is always the closed target format and keeps the default, and the A2 adapters are closed-only (the A2 `runA2Bot` also refuses an open-sheet format at its start). Open-format play still needs the loop's per-game format (slice 5) and the sheet block (slice 3); nothing here makes a policy read the sheet. Tests: `test/team-sheet-binding.test.ts` (both directions on the goldens and on hand-made channels, the canary that no committed or freshly played closed game holds a `|showteam|` line and that a spliced line is refused by `playerChannel`, `readEpisode` and `loadTrainingSet`, an open Episode read and trained on, the harness-only N3 test that each sheet equals the true team field for field in team order with no Stat Points, IVs or nickname, and a scan that no shipped source imports that test or the shipped binding module any team), `tests/ml/reducer/test_sheet.py`, `packages/public-state/test/sheet.test.ts`. **No hash moved** (the interpreter, `readerSha256`, the belief and search identities and the `probe-damage` feature-set hashes are unchanged). Slice-1 review nits folded in: the `team-inventory.ts` default-format comment, the `sheet-variants.ts` pairing sentence, the A2 session guard.

**Opponent sheet block, closed regime** (ADR-025 erratum E4 point 1, ADR-027 section 2 and slice 3, #1272 part 1 and #1151; `packages/schema/contracts/v1/opponent-sheet.schema.json`, `packages/opponent-model/src/sheet.ts`, `packages/showdown-bridge/src/{opponent-sheet,belief-features}.ts`, `packages/team-dataset/src/belief-features.ts`, `packages/schema/python/areneon_contracts/opponent_sheet.py`). What the player's own view makes probable about each opponent preview slot, as a closed, integers-only derived input beside the observation. **Slice split:** ADR-027 puts the contract and the builder in #1151, so the optional `opponent_sheet` field of `PolicyQuery` and `TrainingExample` (a minor change within v1; omitted when absent, never null) is here; the encoder option (#1152), the worker statement in `ready`, the actor attaching the block, the loader recomputing it, `probe-damage/3` and the open regime's `engine-sheet` builder are later parts (the schema and readers already hold the kinds ADR-027 names, so they need no contract change). **Absent keeps every byte:** the digests of every earlier PolicyQuery and TrainingExample sample are pinned (`opponent-sheet.test.ts`); the handshake's `PolicyQuery` schema hash changed, so the `hello` and `ready` samples did (client and worker ship together). **Shape:** `source {kind, hash, refusal?}` (kind `engine-sheet`, `belief`, `revealed-only`, `oracle-diagnostic`; the `TrainingExample` copy is `TrainingOpponentSheet` with `TrainingSheetSource`, which has no `oracle-diagnostic`, so the schema itself refuses an oracle block as training data; same-named defs must be equal across schemas, hence the renamed pair) and one slot per previewed Pokemon: `species`, `brought_ppm` with `brought_source`, `item` (id null means no item), `ability`, `nature` as `{source, entries[{id, ppm}], other_ppm}` (at most 8 entries in the schema, k 4/3/3 in the first feature set) and `moves` as `{source, entries[{id, inclusion_ppm}], slots_known}` (k 8). **Reader rules** (TS `opponentSheetProblem` / `readOpponentSheet`, Python `sheet_problem`, held equal by the corpus; `readPolicyQuery`, `readTrainingExample`, the worker's `read_query` and `StreamChecker` apply them, and a stream carries one sheet `source.hash` or none): entries sorted by ppm descending then id ascending (a null item id last) and unique; `other_ppm` is `max(0, 10^6 - sum)` and the sum is at most `10^6` plus one per entry (each entry is rounded alone); a field of source `unknown` has no entries, `other_ppm` 0 (moves: `slots_known` 0); `revealed` holds one value at `10^6` (moves: four known and four entries at `10^6`), and a move list holds at least `slots_known` entries at `10^6`; `brought_source` `revealed` is `10^6` and `unknown` is 0; the kind fits the regime (`belief`: no `sheet` field; `revealed-only`: no predicted field; `engine-sheet` and `oracle-diagnostic`: every item, ability, nature and move field at `sheet`; only a `belief` block has a refusal, which makes every slot unknown). **Builder** (`opponentSheet(belief, opponent, hash, k)`, engine-free, in `opponent-model`; a pure function of `belief()`'s marginals and the reader's opponent half, which only tells a revealed fact from a predicted one; no sampled world, seed, `topWorlds`, real team or oracle): the Item-Clause-aware slot marginals (`p`-mixed over disguise assignments by the belief), each ppm the exact rational rounded half up (`roundPpm`), zeros dropped before the cut to the top k, then sorted; `nature` is the sum of the named spreads' natures (the prior's "other spread" entry names none, so a prior with only that entry, the generator-matched one, gives a `unknown` nature field); `revealed` only when the reader shows the fact AND the belief has it alone at probability 1 (a received item or ability is no fact; an evolved Mega holds its stone as a revealed item; the Mega holder-ability rule is the belief's); `slots_known` is the number of distinct revealed moves the belief holds at 10^6, at most 4 (a revealed move the belief cannot place, such as a Transform copy, Struggle or a called move, is skipped as the belief skips it; `opponent-sheet-builder/2`, #1310, counted every revealed move before and so emitted a block its own reader refused); `brought_source` is `revealed` for a settled slot. **While an identity-hiding member is unsettled and a position is shown the block is refused with `disguise_unsettled`**. A refused belief (the belief's codes `reader_flagged`, `team_size`, `species_not_in_prior`, `forme_not_in_prior`, `brought_exceeds_picked`, `disguise_assignments_exceeded`, `evidence_has_no_support`, `legality_redraws_exhausted`, `empty_distribution`) is a block with `source.refusal` and every field unknown (`refusedSheet`), counted per code per game (`SheetBuilder.counts()`, `refusedSharePpm`; an experiment with more than 10,000 ppm refused is invalid, E4 point 5); no silent zero and no rule fallback; the run-setup defects (`format_mismatch`, `item_clause_unsupported`, `spread_sampler_required`) propagate. **Identity:** `sheetFeatureSetHash(belief_identity_hash, k)` is SHA-256 of the JCS of `{feature_set: "opponent-sheet/1", opponent_sheet_format, belief_identity_hash, k_item, k_ability, k_nature, k_moves, rounding: "half-up"}` and is the block's `source.hash`; `OPPONENT_SHEET_FORMAT` (`opponent-sheet-builder/2`) is bumped when the builder computes a value differently (E4 names it `BELIEF_FEATURES_FORMAT`, but that name is already the bridge's constant of the `probe-damage-belief/1` world-mean builder, so this one is separate); the reader file, the pin and the usage prior reach the hash through the belief identity. **Ports:** the bridge does not import the opponent model: `BeliefFeaturesPort` (`identity_hash`, `features_hash`, `prior_hash`, `belief_config_hash`, `prior_format_id`, `k`, `sheet({opponent, flags, trapping?})`) is structural, `createSheetBuilder({engine, vocabulary, port, scenario_id})` is the per-game wrapper (it refuses a prior of another format, builds the trapping check of the player's own move request, #1127, trapping on, and counts), and `SheetInput` is `{request, team, state, flags}` (a type test: no opponent team, omniscient log, hidden verdict, record or seed). The composition root is `createBeliefFeatures` in `team-dataset` (`armParts` of `search-worlds.ts` reads and checks the committed generator-matched prior and belief config; the arm is the primary `generator-matched` one, an upper bound calibrated to the generator); it memoises blocks by the canonical evidence projection of the opponent half (`evidenceKey`: what `slotEvidence` reads, the phantom flag, whether any line was flagged; hit points are not in it) and bypasses the memo whenever a trapping check is present (that check depends on the player's own side). **Tests:** `opponent-model/test/sheet.test.ts` (worked values on the support vocabulary, rounding, the cut, sources, the refusals, golden digests of four cases), `hidden.test.ts` (invariance: equal player channels with different unrevealed opponent items, natures, Stat Points, moves 2 to 4 and back Pokemon give byte-identical blocks at every request; negative control with the real sets; canary markers), `showdown-bridge/test/{opponent-sheet,belief-features}.test.ts` and `samples.test.ts` (the corpus: `opponent-sheet/` valid, invalid and reader-invalid, plus the `opponent-sheet` cases of `policy-query` and `training-example`), `team-dataset/test/belief-features.test.ts` (the real model over the committed random-play channels: every block valid, refusals counted, a golden digest of the values, determinism, the memo) and `tests/contracts/test_opponent_sheet.py`, `tests/ml/test_worker_sheet.py` (the Python reader and the worker). The worker accepts a valid block and ignores it (the encoder does not read it yet).

**Opponent sheet in the encoder, the worker and the loop** (ADR-027 slice 4, #1152, #1272 part 2; closed regime). `model.encoder.options.opponent_sheet` is `{feature_set: "opponent-sheet/1", feature_set_hash}` (the hash is `sheetFeatureSetHash`, the block's `source.hash`; layout changing, checkpoint format `"2"`, hash in the config so in `encoder_version`; `TrainingRunConfig` and `CloneRunConfig` carry it, samples `opponent-sheet`, `*-unknown-set`, `*-true`, `*-short-hash`). **Absent keeps every byte** (`TODAY_ENCODER_VERSION` and the checkpoint id are still pinned), and a query that carries `opponent_sheet` under a config without the option is an `EncodingError`, as `candidate_features` are (nothing is read of input the checkpoint was not trained with). **Encoding** (`model/sheet.py`, `Encoder._sheet_columns`, `PolicyNetwork._sheet_sums`), ADR-027 section 2 word for word: "Each field becomes the probability-weighted sum of its entries' embeddings plus `other_ppm` times an "other" embedding, plus a source embedding and the top probability as a scalar; moves are the inclusion-weighted sum; the result is concatenated to that opponent Pokémon's existing row (no extra tokens)." The columns are on every token, filled for an opponent's token from the slot of the same preview position and NONE/0 on own tokens and phantom entries. Categories embedded and concatenated as any column: `sheet_refusal` (the block's code, the same on every opponent token) and a source tag per field (`sheet_{brought,item,ability,nature,moves}_source`, RESERVED + index in `SOURCES`; the source embedding). Entries: `sheet_{item,ability,nature,move}_<j>` hold the ids (item, ability and move vocabularies, so the same embeddings, with their attribute projections, as the token's own item, ability and moves; a null item id is NONE, the vocabulary's none row; a nature has no vocabulary in the engine-data table, so it is a SHA-256 bucket of 4096 with its own learned table) and the numeric columns `sheet_*_ppm` / `sheet_{item,ability,nature}_other` hold `ppm / 10^6`; the network embeds each id, multiplies by its weight, sums over the entries, adds `other` times a learned per-field embedding (`sheet_other`; moves have none) and concatenates the four sums to the token's categories before the token layer. These weight columns are NOT inputs of the numeric layer. Scalars (the numeric layer's only sheet inputs): `sheet_brought` (brought_ppm over 10^6) and the top probability of each field (`sheet_{item,ability,nature,moves}_top`, the largest entry over 10^6). `slots_known` is not encoded (a revealed move is an entry at 10^6). An unknown field has no entries and no `other` mass (the reader rule), so its sum is zero and its source tag says why. Fail closed (`EncodingError`): no block under the option, another `source.hash`, a slot count other than the non-phantom opponent roster, a slot species other than the reducer's `base_species` of that entry, an id the engine data lacks, more entries than k. The constants (k 4/3/3/8, closed words, bucket count, the encoding rule) are hashed into `encoder_version`. **Worker:** `ready` gains the optional `opponent_sheet` statement (`PolicyHandshake`, a minor change within v1; the real worker states it from its checkpoints' model config), `read_query` takes `SheetSupport(source, diagnostic)`: a block goes only with a stated source and must carry its hash (a worker that states none refuses a block, one that states one refuses a query without), the reader rules apply, and an `oracle-diagnostic` block is an `invalid_query` unless the worker was started with `--diagnostic-sheet` (a labelled, test-only diagnostic run, ADR-027 section 7; the TrainingExample schema has no such kind at all). **Client:** `PolicyWorkerOptions.sheet` is the source this side can supply; a worker that states another source, or any when this is absent, is a `PolicyHandshakeError` before any battle; `client.sheetSource` is the statement and `ask` refuses a block to a worker that stated none, no block to one that did, and a block of another source. **Seat:** `PolicySeat.sheet` / `PolicyActorOptions.sheet` `{builder, record?}` attach the block to each query (Team Preview and forced switches included) only when the worker stated a source, built by `sheetForChannel(builder, {player, lines, team})`, the one function that reads the player's own lines with the shared reader, takes the request the channel ends in, the reader's opponent half and flags and builds (the trapping check of the player's own move request is on); a builder refusal is the `unknown` block with its code, counted in the builder, never a forfeit and never a rule fallback; a block of another hash than the stated one is a `policy_protocol` incident; a defect of the run reaches `guardActor` (a `crash` forfeit). **Loader:** `TrainingInput.sheet` (`SheetSetup`) recomputes every example's block with `sheetForChannel` from the Episode's own channel prefix and own team, one builder per Episode, byte-equal to what was sent (`team-dataset/test/sheet-transport.test.ts` plays a seeded game through `policyGame` and compares each query's block with the loader's; an oracle block is never training data). The loader recomputes when no play-time sidecar is present; the sidecar the seat's `record` hook feeds is the next paragraph (#1315, which EXP-B1's cost gate asked for). **Run wiring:** `sheetSetup(engine, config, factory)` (`league-generation.ts`) builds the port through the composition root's `BeliefFeaturesFactory` (the bridge does not import the model), refuses a config whose named hash this checkout does not compute and a script with no factory, and gives the client its statement and the seats and the loader their builder; `runGenerationCli` takes the factory as its last argument and `team-dataset/scripts/play-generation.ts` passes `createBeliefFeatures`; the per-process counts of blocks and refusals by code go to stderr at the end of a generation (they are NOT in the run files yet: the run-record fields and the dashboard are the experiment's wiring, #1273). `learner/data.py` passes the example's block to the encoder. **Evaluation seats and counts:** see the next paragraph. The search actor still needs a pair without the option (`playProgress` refuses a sheet pair with a search candidate), and the policy id has no `sheet` member (the checkpoints' ids and `encoder_version` already name the source). Tests: `tests/ml/model/test_sheet.py`, `tests/ml/learner/test_sheet.py`, `tests/ml/test_worker_sheet.py`, `tests/ml/progress/test_sheet.py`, `showdown-bridge/test/sheet-handshake.test.ts`, `team-dataset/test/sheet-transport.test.ts`, `sheet-invariance.test.ts` (blocks through the real port, the real trapping check and `sheetForChannel` are byte-identical at every request with an equal player channel whatever the opponent holds unrevealed; a port handed the real team differs). **A diagnostic (oracle) run shares the stated source and the policy id:** `--diagnostic-sheet` only lets the worker read an `oracle-diagnostic` block, whose `source.hash` must equal the stated one, and a diagnostic run's seats play under the same checkpoints and so the same policy id as an ordinary run; the run is labelled outside the policy id (by its command line and the records that name it), never by the id. **Play-time opponent sheet sidecar** (#1315, ADR-027; `packages/showdown-bridge/src/sheet-recompute.ts`, the sheet's twin of the rows sidecar of #1271). Tests: `team-dataset/test/sheet-generation.test.ts` (files written, stream from the sidecar equals the recomputed and the play-time stream, the three-way equality is the independence assertion; the sidecar is trusted like the rows sidecar, so that equality is the guard, and a tampered file changing the stream only shows the files are read; other source/stale/damaged files are recomputed; a failing write is skipped), `showdown-bridge/test/sheet-recompute.test.ts` (every reader branch, the writer's gap and wrong-source cases), `team-dataset/test/generation-cli-sheet.test.ts` (two game processes write the single-process stream). **Opponent sheet in the evaluations and the run records** (ADR-025 erratum E4 point 5, ADR-027 slice 5, #1272 part 3). **Seats:** the progress evaluation, the head to head and the private test build the block like the training seats: `pairSheetSetup(engine, config, factory)` (`progress-cli.ts`, a usage error when this checkout does not compute the hash the config names or when the script has no factory) is made from EACH run's own config, so a sheet pair against a no-sheet pair gives each seat its own statement and its own block or none (the client option `sheet` of `frozenPairClient`; `ProgressOptions.sheet`, `versus.sheet`, `PrivateTestOptions.sheet`, `opponent_pair.sheet`; the previous generation, the anchors and the ablation's Previews are the run's own pair and get the run's setup). `ProgressPorts.belief_features` / `PrivateTestSources.belief_features` is the composition root's factory (`team-dataset/scripts/progress.ts`, `private-team-store/scripts/private-test.ts`). A fresh `SheetBuilder` per game and seat. The Python `_source` no longer refuses a pair that reads `opponent_sheet`. **Counts in the run files:** `sheet-counts.ts` (pure) holds `SheetCounts` `{requests, refused, refusals[{code, count}]}` (refusals sorted by code and unique, adding up to `refused`, at most `requests`), `sumSheetCounts`, `refusedSharePpm` and `MAX_REFUSED_PPM` (10,000 = 1%). A training game row of `generation.json` gains the optional `sheet` (the pair's seats' builders summed; absent for a pair without the option, so every other run keeps its bytes; the inspector's `GAME_KEYS` has it as an optional closed key, `read_sheet_counts` in `loop/sheet_counts.py` checks it). The progress record v2 gains `GameRow.sheet` and `Match.sheet` (a minor change within v2: the CANDIDATE seat's counts per game and their sum per match; a match carries it exactly when every row does; readers in TS `checkSheetOfMatch` and Python `_check_sheet_of_match`; corpus `sheet-match` and the `sheet-*` reader-invalid and invalid samples). **Where the counts appear, exactly:** (1) `generation.json`, each game row of a training generation (`sheet`: the pair's seats summed); (2) the progress record v2 (`progress.json`): each game row (`games[].sheet`) and each match (`matches[].sheet`, the sum), candidate seat only; (3) the dashboard: the training section "Opponent sheet blocks" (per generation) and the section "Opponent sheet blocks in the progress evaluation" (per evaluated match); (4) `summary_of`'s per-match `sheet` object and the progress command's printed result (`matches[].sheet` and a top-level `sheet_status {valid, invalid_matches}`); (5) the generation process's stderr line. **E4's validity rule is exposed, not enforced, and decided exactly:** a refused share above 1% invalidates the experiment, tested as `refused * 100 > requests` on the integers in both languages (`SheetCounts.valid`, `sheetCountsValid`); `refused_share_ppm` is floored and is for display only. **Sheet regimes: a run that trains on open-sheet and closed-sheet games** (ADR-027 sections 5 and 5a, ADR-010 erratum A8, #1153; `packages/showdown-bridge/src/sheet-regimes.ts`, `ml/areneon_ml/loop/sheet_regimes.py`). `TrainingRunConfig.sheet_regimes {open_format, closed_share_ppm, open_only_generations, closed_sheet_source}` is optional (a minor change within v1; an older reader refuses it); `format_id` stays the closed (target) format. **Absent keeps every byte.** What is byte-pinned: `league-generation-regimes.test.ts` rewrites the committed `fixtures/loop/inspector-run` generations' `generation.json`, `plan.json` and `league-run.json` with `writeGeneration` (and `planGeneration`/`buildLeagueRunRecord`) and compares them to the committed files, and `test_sheet_regimes.py` rebuilds the committed population fixture's CheckpointRecords with `checkpoint_record` and compares their JCS bytes. The stream, `training-set.json` and the Episodes depend on trained weights or an engine run and are not compared with a fixture; the loader change only adds a check and the test shows equal stream bytes with and without it. **Plan:** `planGeneration` adds `format_id` to every entry (only with the field): a generation `g <= open_only_generations` plays all its self-play games in `open_format`, later ones exactly `round(games * closed_share_ppm / 10^6)` (half up, integers) closed, the games with the lowest `regimeDigest` = SHA-256 over the JCS of `{run_key, generation, game, purpose: "sheet-regime/1"}` (ties by game index; the Python twin `self_play_regimes` is held equal by pinned vectors and by `check_generation_plan`, which refuses a plan whose formats are not the quota). Both seats of a game share its format. **Decisions where the ADR left room:** the quota is over the self-play games only; a baseline game is always closed, even in an open-only generation (the damage-aware mechanics config exists for the closed format only; `checkPlanRegimes` refuses an open-format baseline game), and it counts as closed in `regimes`; generation numbers are the plan's (from 1), so `open_only_generations: 10` makes generations 1 to 10 open. **Readers** (TS `checkSheetRegimes`, Python `_sheet_regimes`): `open_format` differs from `format_id`; `closed_sheet_source: "belief"` is refused until slice 6 builds it; a population, `generation.search_labels` and the `opponent_sheet` encoder option are refused with it (the open regime's `engine-sheet` source and the closed regime's `revealed-only` source are later slices, so this slice drives formats, plan and records only). That `format_id`'s rules record is `closed`, `open_format`'s is `open` and `checkSheetVariants` passes is asked of the engine where one is loaded: `resolveRegimes(engine, config)` resolves both formats once per generation (called by `playEntries` and `playGeneration`) and returns `{closed, open, sorted}` manifests. **Records:** (baseline games are always closed and count as closed in `regimes`.) `LeagueRunRecord` (the schema has no manifest field of its own) gains optional `regimes {open, closed}` (sum to `games`, equal the plan's counts) and `game_manifests`, `CheckpointRecord` gains optional `game_manifests` (`manifest`, the closed one, must be in it); both are exactly the two formats' manifests (one each, one engine commit and regulation, `format_id` and `open_format` named by the config where it is known: TS `checkLeagueRunRecord`, Python `manifests_problem`) sorted by JCS bytes (the decision: always both, not only those a generation happened to play, so `manifest` is always among them), written together or not at all and only for a config with `sheet_regimes`; `generation.json` carries `game_manifests` (the Python driver checks it and the league record agree and passes it to `RecordFacts`/`Sealing`); the Python record readers check the structure (sorted, `manifest` among them, one engine commit and regulation); the engine-side variant check is Node's. `team-set.json`, `known_synthetic` and `readDataset` stay on the closed format only. Each battle runs and validates under its plan entry's format, so every Episode carries its game's own manifest. **Loader:** `TrainingInput.formats` (set by `playGeneration` for a mixed run) refuses an Episode whose manifest names another format, and an open-format Episode when the run reads opponent sheet blocks (their `engine-sheet` source is not built; `playOne` refuses to play such a game too); without the option the loader is unchanged. The inspector's readers accept the optional `format_id` plan key and `game_manifests` (they still show the closed format only). Tests: `sheet-regimes.test.ts`, `league-generation-regimes.test.ts` (a tiny mixed generation with the real engine: formats, counts, manifests, rerun byte-identical, loader), `tests/ml/loop/test_sheet_regimes.py` (the twin, the readers, a fake-player loop with both formats' manifests in every record, resume), and the corpus samples `*sheet-regimes*`, `*game-manifests*`, `league-run-record/*regimes*`. **Limits:** the ticket's loader recompute of `engine-sheet` open blocks is deferred until #1151 (PR #352) merges (the `engine-sheet` builder is not on main). The engine-side `checkSheetVariants` runs in Node only (`resolveRegimes`); the record readers check structure only. A Battle-only `warm_start` leaves generation 0's clone records without `game_manifests` (they are the cloning run's records, kept byte for byte, and are not rewritten), so a mixed run's generation 0 can hold a clone record without the field while later records have it. `feature-service.ts` derives the regime of the channels it builds from the run's closed `format_id` (`spec.format_id`), so an offline recompute through that service of an open-format Episode's rows would read an open channel as closed: the training loader derives it from each Episode's own manifest and is right, the service is not used by the loop (latent). **Sheet builders without a belief: `engine-sheet/1` and `revealed-only/1`** (ADR-027 slice 3, #1151; `packages/showdown-bridge/src/sheet-builders.ts`). The contract, reader rules and the belief builder are the paragraphs above; this adds the two builders that need no belief, both pure functions of the player's own view. `sheetFromChannel(channel: PlayerChannel, record)` is the only constructor of an `engine-sheet` block (the open regime): it reads the channel with the shared reader and turns the opponent's engine-sent `|showteam|` sheet (`PublicSide.sheet`) into one slot per preview Pokemon with item, ability, nature and every move at source `sheet` and 1,000,000 ppm (`slots_known` is the move count, a null item is an entry with a null id), `brought_ppm` 0 with source `unknown` (the belief fills it in slice 6). It refuses (`SheetBuildError`, fixed words) a record whose `team_sheets` is not `open`, a channel that does not end in a request, one the reader flagged, one with no opponent sheet, a sheet Pokemon with no nature or with a repeated or more than four moves. `revealedOnlySheet(opponent, flags)` is the closed regime's `revealed-only/1` block of the reader's opponent half: an item (or the Mega stone of an evolved Mega) and an ability the reader shows and the Pokemon did not receive or copy are `revealed` at 1,000,000 ppm; the nature, `brought_ppm` and everything else are `unknown`; a move field is `revealed` only with all four moves shown (the contract's revealed move field is complete) and `unknown` otherwise, since revealed-only has no predicted source (the revealed moves stay in the observation's log), and a Transformed Pokemon's moves are unknown (a fifth shown move, Struggle's, also leaves the list unknown). It fails closed like the belief: any reader flag, or a phantom (Illusion twin) roster entry, is a `SheetBuildError` `reader_flagged`, so its slots are exactly the non-phantom roster. `sheetFromChannel` also refuses a sheet whose length differs from the roster's and a roster entry without a species (a sheet that arrives before the `|poke|` lines), as a `SheetBuildError`. The regime comes from the format's rules record, never from the channel. Slots are named by the reader's `base_species`, as the belief block's refusal does. `source.hash` is the SHA-256 of the JCS of `{feature_set, sheet_builders_format}` (`ENGINE_SHEET_HASH`, `REVEALED_ONLY_HASH`; `SHEET_BUILDERS_FORMAT` `sheet-builders/1`, bumped when either builder computes a value differently). Neither builder is wired into a seat, the loader or the run config yet (slice 5 part 1, #1153, and the evaluation wiring, #1157); the module imports only the shared reader, the sheet types, `canonical-json.ts`, `rules.ts` and Node's SHA-256. Tests (`test/sheet-builders.test.ts`): the engine-sheet block equals the true opponent team field for field at every request of the open-regime corpus; the open-regime block is invariant to the opponent's Stat Points with a negative control (a changed nature changes it); the revealed-only block's known fields are the true ones, equal under changed unrevealed opponent fields on seeded replays, differ for a builder handed a real hidden field, and are equal for the A1 and A2 forms of every `a2-pairs.json` decision; the type test (a team, an omniscient log, a battle record, a seed or hidden verdicts are compile errors); a source scan; an oracle block in a `TrainingExample` is refused by `readTrainingExample`.

**Open-sheet matches in the progress record** (ADR-027 section 5, 5a and 8, ADR-017 erratum E4 items 1 and 2, #1154; `packages/showdown-bridge/src/progress-evaluation.ts` `ProgressOptions.open_format`, `progress-cli.ts` `--team-sheets`, `progress-records.ts` (`OPEN_PREFIX`, `checkTeamSheetsOfMatch`, `checkGameManifests`), `ml/areneon_ml/progress/team_sheets.py`, `run.py`, `report/`). `python -m areneon_ml.loop progress --team-sheets open` (Node `--team-sheets open`; `open` is the only value, closed is what an evaluation plays without it) plays, beside every closed match, the OPEN TWIN of each match against a scripted baseline (`actor-rule`, `random-legal`, the opt-in damage-aware baseline, extra play modes included) and against another run's pair (`--versus-run-dir`): `open-<closed match id>`, same pairs, seeds, seats and policy ids, in the engine's open-sheet variant of the run's format. The open format is not a flag value: the command takes the one open format the rules snapshot pairs with the run's closed format (`sheetVariantPairs`; none or several is a usage error) and `playProgress` re-checks it (`checkSheetVariants`, the run's format closed and the other open) and resolves its manifest. **Contract (a minor change within version 2; absent keeps every existing record's bytes and hash, a reader older than the fields rejects such a record):** a match gains the optional `team_sheets` (`"open"` for an open match; the schema also lists `"closed"` for the ticket's union but writers never state it and both readers refuse it, so absent is the one spelling of closed) and the record the optional `game_manifests` (exactly two version manifests, the closed one in `version_manifest` and the open format's, sorted by JCS bytes, two formats of one engine commit and regulation), present exactly when some match is open. A match's games use the manifest its `team_sheets` names: an open stored game's `version_manifest` is the open one (checked by the Python run verification, `verify_progress_dir` and the replay reader through `game_manifest(record, match)`); the open games' omniscient logs hold the engine's `|showteam|` lines. Reader rules (TS and Python, held equal by the corpus): an open match is named `open-<name>` exactly when it carries `team_sheets: open`, plays a baseline or another run's pair (never an anchor, previous generation, search, solver, ablation or sheet-block match: a pair that reads `opponent_sheet` is refused open here, the open block's builder is #1157, slice 7), and `game_manifests` follows the rule above; the Python evaluation (`check_team_sheets`) additionally requires the open matches to be exactly the open twins of the closed baseline and cross-run matches, played like them (policy id, opponent, mode, games, play settings, one baseline identity). **Never pooled:** an open match has its own match row, interval and chart (`Score vs open-<opponent>`), the closed checks written before the option read `closed_view(record)` (the record without its open matches), the play-mode gap pairs open argmax with open sample (the stem carries the prefix), and the per-generation row (`team_sheets_view`, in the printed result as `team_sheets` and on the dashboard as "Closed versus open team sheets") puts the score against the damage-aware baseline in the closed and in the open format side by side, each with its own pair-level interval, with no difference and no combined number (derived, never stored). Decisions where the ticket left room: (1) open twins, not open-only matches, so one record always holds the closed (target) view the primary progress view stays; (2) the damage-aware baseline plays the open battles with its closed-format records (the mechanics config exists only for the closed format; the actor reads the engine's `|showteam|` lines through the shared reader's sheet layer, and its identity hash is the closed one, so a record keeps one identity); an open-format baseline with its own mechanics config stays a later slice (ADR-027 section 5a); (3) a candidate pair that reads candidate features plays the open battles with its closed feature set (the rows are on assumed sets, not on the sheets); (4) open matches are not played with anchors, a previous generation, the search, solver or Preview ablation candidates (refused up front, TS and Python); (5) the engine data of a replay page is the closed format's (the open format has no fixture directory of its own), read from the progress record's manifest; the replay page does not label the format yet. A committed progress generation is kept only for the same kind of request (a record with open matches is never rewritten, and a request for them against a record without is refused: use a progress directory of its own).
